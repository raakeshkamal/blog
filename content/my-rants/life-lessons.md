## Investing Lessons
- Only rules to make money - Be the first or Be the smart or Cheat
- Only invest in public companies
- Markets can stay irrational for longer than you can stay solvent
- Only invest in low-cost well diversified ETFs for the long-term
- If you ever decide to invest to take a gamble on a risky trade like meme-stocks or crypto, be ready to lose all your money
- If you hold both taxed and tax-free account, rebalance using the tax-free account and leave the taxed account only for new funds(sort of rebalancing) 
- Less regulated private companies & crypto provide easy way for you to get burned (with trump's recent changes to allow 401k to invest in private funds and crypto) - They call it democratization 😂
- Figure out portfolio allocation that you are comfortable with and keep rebalancing
  - rebalancing helps you keep taking profits when there is upward movement and helps you buy the dip on the downward movement
  - its a sort of manual mean reversal approach
