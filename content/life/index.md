##Financial Adivice
- Only invest in public companies 
- Less regulated private companies provide easy way for you to get burned (with trump's recent changes to allow 401k to invest in private companies) - They call it democratization
- Markets can stay irrational for longer than you can stay solvent
- Only invest in low-cost well diversified ETFs for the long-term
- If you ever decide to invest to take a gamble on a risky trade like meme-stocks or crypto, be ready to lose all your money
- If you hold both taxed and tax-free account, rebalance using the tax-free account and leave the taxed account only for new funds(sort of rebalancing)
